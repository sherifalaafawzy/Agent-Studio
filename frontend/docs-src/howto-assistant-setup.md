**You will need:** a model for the assistant (Settings, Assistant) and the assistant switched on.

## Steps

1. Go to the screen you want help with, for example **Agent Studio** or **Settings**.
2. Open the assistant (the button in the corner) and say what you want: "help me create an agent for summarising meeting notes".
3. The assistant reads the headings, buttons and form fields on the screen. It cannot see passwords or keys.
4. It may **walk you through** with highlighted cards, **open the documentation** beside you, or **propose values** for form fields.
5. For form changes a card appears that shows each field **before and after**. Press **Apply** to accept or **Not now** to refuse. Nothing changes until you press Apply.
6. Review the form yourself and press the screen's own save button. The assistant fills fields, it does not save for you.

## Things it will not do

- Fill in a field that holds a secret (a password, key, token or similar). It tells you to type it yourself.
- Change a field that is read-only.
- Take actions you have switched off in Settings, Assistant.
