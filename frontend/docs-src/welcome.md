Agent Studio is a workbench on your own PC for designing, running and watching AI agents. An **agent** is an AI model with a job, some tools, and instructions about how to work. You can chat with one, give it your documents, let it use other programs, link several together, run them on a schedule of steps (workflows), build small apps on top of them, or talk to them out loud.

Everything stays on this PC unless you choose an online model or a cloud service. Online models cost money, so every provider has a **spend limit** that is checked *before* each request. Local models (through Ollama) are free.

![The chat screen: sidebar on the left, the message box at the bottom](shot:chat-home)

## What you can do

- **Chat** with a plain model or one of your agents, side by side with a [canvas](doc:canvas) for editing documents and code together. See [Chat](doc:chat).
- **Build agents** with a guided wizard or an advanced editor. See [Agents](doc:agents).
- **Give agents knowledge**: your own documents, made searchable. See [Knowledge](doc:knowledge).
- **Give agents abilities** with [MCP servers](doc:mcp-servers) and [skills](doc:skills).
- **Automate** with [Workflows](doc:workflows), boxes and arrows with branches, approvals and an API importer.
- **Build apps** that use your agents. See [Apps](doc:apps).
- **Research** with the [Science Lab](doc:science-lab).
- **Talk** to an agent, voice only or with an avatar. See [Live mode](doc:live-mode).
- **Keep it safe and visible** with [security checks](doc:security), [telemetry](doc:telemetry) and [spend limits](doc:billing-and-limits).

## Where to begin

1. Open the [set-up guide](doc:first-steps). It checks your PC and gets you to a first working agent.
2. Read [The building blocks](doc:concepts) once. It explains the words used everywhere.
3. Pick a recipe from the how-to pages, for example [Build your first agent](doc:howto-first-agent).

> **Tip:** you can open this documentation beside whatever you are doing. Press **Ctrl+/** (or **?** when no text box is selected), or use the Docs tab of the assistant. See [The assistant and these docs](doc:assistant-and-docs).

## How to find your way around

The sidebar groups the app into **Work** (dashboard, chat, live, runs, artifacts, external agents), **Build** (agents, workflows, apps, science, skills, knowledge, MCP servers, catalog, memory) and **Observe** (telemetry, billing, security, evaluations, AI-BOM, settings). **Ctrl+K** opens a quick jump box for any page or recent chat.
