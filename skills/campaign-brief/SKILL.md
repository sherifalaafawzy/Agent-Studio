# Campaign brief

**When to use:** someone needs a plan for a launch, promotion or content push.

1. Pin down inputs: goal (one measurable outcome), audience, offer, budget, dates, constraints (brand, legal, regions). Ask for what is missing; state defaults you assume.
2. Write the brief: **Objective + KPI + target**, **Audience & insight**, **Key message** (one sentence + three proof points), **Channels** (why each, budget %), **Calendar** (week by week), **Assets needed**, **Measurement** (metric, source, owner, review date), **Risks**.
3. Make the KPI testable: baseline, target, time window. If analytics are connected, pull the baseline instead of guessing.
4. Keep claims honest: no invented statistics, testimonials or competitor numbers; mark any placeholder clearly.

**Safety:** drafts only - do not publish, schedule or spend. Ad spend and sends need a human decision.
