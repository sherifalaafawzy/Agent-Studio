# Lead qualification

**When to use:** a new lead (form fill, list, CRM record) needs a fit score and a next action.

1. Ask for or load the ideal customer profile (industry, size, region, tech, budget range). If none exists, propose one and say it is an assumption.
2. Collect only public/business facts: company, role, size, what they asked for. Use web search for company facts; cite the source for each claim. Never guess personal data.
3. Score each criterion 0-2 with a reason: **Fit**, **Need** (a stated problem), **Authority** (decision maker / influencer / unknown), **Timing**. Total /8 and a label: hot (7-8), warm (4-6), nurture (0-3).
4. Recommend one next step and a short draft message the rep can edit. Mention what is missing to qualify further (the single best question to ask).
5. Output a compact card: score, reasons, evidence links, next step, draft.

**Safety:** read-only by default. Do not create or update CRM records, send email or contact anyone - propose, a human acts. Respect privacy: no scraping of personal social profiles.
