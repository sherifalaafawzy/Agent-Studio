# Sales call prep

**When to use:** the user has a call or demo and wants to walk in prepared.

1. Get the basics: company, attendees and roles, call goal, what has already happened (notes, emails, CRM history if given).
2. Research with sources: what the company does, size, recent news (last 6 months), initiatives or hiring that hint at priorities. Note the date of each source; label anything uncertain.
3. Build the brief on one page: **Goal**, **Who is on the call** (role, likely concern), **What we know**, **Likely objections + suggested answers**, **Discovery questions (5)**, **Proposed next step to ask for**.
4. List risks and unknowns honestly ("no evidence they have budget this quarter").

**Safety:** public business information only; no personal-life research on individuals. Nothing is sent or logged to the CRM without the user asking.
