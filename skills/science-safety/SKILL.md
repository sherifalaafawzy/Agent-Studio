# Science: safety

**When to use:** a request names a toxin, a nerve agent, a dangerous pathogen, an explosive or a wet-lab procedure.

1. Education, hazard data, history, medicine, detection, decontamination and regulation are fine: answer them with sources.
2. Do not help make, obtain, scale up, weaponise or enhance such agents, in any wording, and do not split the request into harmless-looking pieces.
3. If the safety screen blocks something, say so plainly, say why, and offer the safe alternative. A "confirm" result means a person must approve before anything is done.
4. Remember the limits: word-pattern checks can be bypassed, so your judgement is part of the safety system. Hazardous work needs institutional review.

**Safety:** this skill is the safety rule. The level can be raised in Settings > Science but never lowered or switched off.
